## New

- Settings is now four steps - Authorisation, Tables, Files and Filtering - each collapsing once it's done.
- **Choose from Drive** picks a spreadsheet from your Google Drive, and **New spreadsheet** makes one named after the project. On the command line, `tracker choose-spreadsheet` picks one.
- A Linux app (an AppImage), which updates itself like the Mac and Windows apps.
- **Show in Finder** (or Explorer) beside each tracked folder.
- **Reset settings**, at the bottom of Settings.
- While watching, a progress bar along **Stop watching** shows the wait until the next scan.
- After a dry run, click new, modified, deleted or unchanged to filter to just those.

## Improved

- Google no longer warns that it hasn't checked the app when you sign in.
- Settings shows the base's or spreadsheet's name rather than its ID.
- Sheets it creates keep the header row and `_path` in view, and fit their columns to what's in them the first time.
- **Run once** and **Start watching** go to **Activity**, a dry run to **Tracked**, and a failed run shows why.
- The app opens on Settings when it hasn't been configured.
- Each filtering rule has a tooltip that says what it does.

## Fixed

- Closing the browser tab while signing in no longer leaves the button greyed out: sign in again, or **Cancel**.
